Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

A+ Trades ("A+ Trades", "we", "us") is operated by an individual, Jakub O'Meally, based in Poland. This policy explains what data A+ Trades collects, why, and what rights you have over it.

Data controller: Jakub O'Meally

Contact: aplustradesjournal@gmail.com

1. What data we collect

When you create an account and use A+ Trades, we collect:

  • Account data: your email address, password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and the nickname you choose to show friends.
  • Trading data you enter: trades, payouts, journal entries, moods, notes, confluences, targets, patterns, and any photos or videos you attach to a trade.
  • Billing data: if you subscribe, Stripe collects and stores your payment details directly — we never see or store your card number. We only keep your subscription status and Stripe customer ID so the app knows whether your account is active.
  • Usage data your browser already sends: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and debugging.

2. Why we process it (legal basis)

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account, save your trading data, and run the subscription you sign up for.
  • Legitimate interest — to keep the service secure, debug issues, and prevent abuse.
  • Consent — for anything optional, like appearing on the public leaderboard, which is opt-in and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

3. Who else sees your data

We use a small number of infrastructure providers (sub-processors) to run A+ Trades. None of them may use your data for their own purposes.

  • Supabase, Inc. (database, authentication, file storage) — stores your account and trading data. Your trades, journal entries, and media are private to your account by default; only opt-in features (Friends, Groups, the leaderboard) share specific, limited data with other users.
  • Stripe, Inc. (payments) — processes subscription billing. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Vercel Inc. (hosting) — serves the app and its serverless functions, and sees standard request logs (IP address, timestamps).
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts) — your browser loads the app's typeface directly from Google's font servers (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which means Google receives your IP address for that request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. and jsDelivr — deliver the open-source code libraries (React, Babel, Supabase's client library) the app runs on. Your browser fetches these directly from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net, which likewise receive your IP address for that request.

These providers may process data outside Poland/the EEA (e.g. in the United States). Where that happens, they do so under their own GDPR-compliant safeguards (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) — see each provider's own privacy policy for details.

We never sell your data, and we never share your individual trades with anyone unless you explicitly choose to (accepting a friend, joining a group, or opting into the leaderboard).

4. Automated processing & AI

A+ Trades itself was built with the help of AI coding tools (Anthropic's Claude). Separately, inside the app:

  • Journal entries are automatically, locally checked against a fixed list of crisis-related keywords (e.g. mentions of suicide or self-harm) so we can show you crisis-support resources if you write something concerning. This is simple keyword matching, not an AI model, doesn't diagnose or judge anything, is not sent anywhere or reviewed by a person, and never blocks or changes what you save.
  • We don't currently use AI to read, analyze, or generate insights from your trading data. If that changes, we'll describe it here before it ships.

5. Cookies & local storage

A+ Trades does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your login session is kept in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so you stay signed in. If you use Stripe Checkout to subscribe, Stripe may set its own cookies on stripe.com during that process — that's covered by Stripe's own policy, not ours.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and trading data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (or ask us to), we delete your data within 30 days, except where we're required to keep billing records for tax/legal reasons.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most of this you can already do yourself in the app).
  • Request deletion of your account and data ("right to be forgotten") — this includes any photos or videos you've uploaded. You can delete individual trade photos/videos yourself at any time directly in the app; ask us by email for full account deletion.
  • Export your data — the app already has a built-in Export function for your trades, or you can ask us for a full copy.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for anything consent-based (e.g. the leaderboard).
  • Lodge a complaint with Poland's data protection authority, UODO, or your own country's equivalent.

To exercise any of these, email aplustradesjournal@gmail.com.

8. Children

A+ Trades is not directed at, and is not intended for, anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email.
